Good to know

Got questions?

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. Your first session is free, so you can see the equipment, meet the trainers and feel the atmosphere before joining.

Are trainers included in the membership?

Floor trainers guide everyone on safe form. Dedicated personal training is available as an add-on and included in the yearly plan.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely. We start with a fitness assessment and build a plan that matches your level, age and goal.

Do you have separate batches for women?

Yes, we run women-only batches and have a trained female coach on every shift.

Can I pause my membership?

Quarterly and yearly members can freeze their plan for up to 30 days for travel or medical reasons.
