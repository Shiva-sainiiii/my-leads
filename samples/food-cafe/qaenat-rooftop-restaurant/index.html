A garden in the sky, above Model Town

Qaenat opened in 2021 on the rooftop of Dhouchak Complex, near Tikona Park. Green walls, white arches and warm lamps make the terrace feel like a garden that happens to be five floors up.

Families come for dinner, couples come for the sunset and the city lights, and friends book the terrace for birthdays and kitty parties. When the sun goes down, the live singer starts.

Meet the chef

Passion, perfection and a taste you do not forget.

Chef Padam Singh leads the Qaenat kitchen. He is known for the Qaenat Special Paneer, the slow-cooked Qaenat Dal and the Murgh Hara Pyaza, and his approach to cooking is built on passion and perfection.
