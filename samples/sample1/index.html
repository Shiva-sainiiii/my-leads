Our Journey

A century on Railway Road

From a single counter in Old Anaj Mandi to one of North India's largest jewellery destinations — scroll to follow our story.

1920

Lala Ghasi Ram opens the first counter near Old Anaj Mandi Chowk, Rohtak, trading in gold ornaments for local families.

1948

The second generation takes over, expanding into bridal sets as Rohtak's wedding trade grows.

1975

Introduction of fine Meenakari and Polki work, building the house's reputation for bridal craftsmanship.

1998

Launch of the dedicated 92.5 Sterling Silver showroom — soon regarded as the finest in Haryana.

2010

Adoption of 100% BIS Hallmarking across all gold inventory, ahead of it becoming an industry norm.

Today

A multi-generational legacy and one of the oldest, largest jewellery destinations in North India.
