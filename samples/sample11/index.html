About the School

Since 2013

S.D. Memorial Convent Sr. Sec. School is run by the Shri Giri Raj Education Society, founded in memory of Late Sh. K.L. Khurana. We are an English-medium, co-educational day school affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi, currently accredited for the period 01/04/2024 to 31/03/2029.

Our vision is simple: a child's intelligence is not bound by the social standing, caste or gender of their parents. Every student who walks through our gates gets the same chance to learn, question and grow — on a quiet, two-acre green campus built for exactly that.

Beyond the classroom, students take part in public speaking, drama, music, dance and fine arts, alongside sports like basketball on campus.

  • Computer Lab
  • Well-stocked Library
  • Activity-based Learning
  • Basketball & Sports Ground
  • Public Speaking & Drama

Admissions

Nursery – Class 11

We admit students from Nursery through Class 11 each session. Age and class eligibility follow CBSE and Haryana state norms. Bring the documents below to the school office to begin the process.

  1. Transfer Certificate (for students joining from another school)
  2. Birth Certificate (original + photocopy)
  3. Aadhaar Card of the student
  4. Previous year's report card / mark sheet
  5. Passport-size photographs (student and parents)

Have questions about admission?

Call the campus office directly — our team can walk you through eligibility, fees and the next intake dates.
