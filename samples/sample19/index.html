About the property

A well-kept room, in the one part of town everyone already knows how to find.

Hotel Landlord Premier sits two floors above Anand Plaza, opposite Pillar No. 15 and above Bandhan Bank — the kind of address a rickshaw driver in Rohtak doesn't need typed out twice. Inside, it's warm teak panelling, quiet air-conditioning, and a front desk that answers the phone at 2 a.m. as readily as at 2 p.m.

We built this for the guest who wants a clean, secure room without paying five-star prices for it — couples, families passing through, and the business traveller who has a 7 a.m. train to catch.
