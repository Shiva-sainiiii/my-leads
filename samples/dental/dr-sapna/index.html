Questions patients often ask

What is microscope-assisted dentistry?

A high-magnification dental microscope gives the dentist a far clearer view of your tooth, so treatment can be more precise and detailed.

Can a badly damaged tooth be saved?

Often, yes. Dr. Sapna performs microscope-assisted root canals and retreatments (Re-RCT). She will examine the tooth first and tell you what is possible.

Will the treatment hurt?

The clinic's gentle, light-handed approach is built around comfort, and patients regularly describe their treatment as pain-free.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended. We are open Monday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Book with the form below, on WhatsApp or by phone.
