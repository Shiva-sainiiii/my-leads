Questions patients often ask.

Will the treatment hurt?

Our approach is built around comfort. Procedures are done gently, with each step explained before we begin.

Can a root canal be done in one sitting?

Often, yes, using rotary endodontics. Dr. Vipin will examine the tooth first and tell you what is possible.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so you are seen without waiting. For a dental emergency, call us directly.

Is emergency care available?

Yes. The hospital runs emergency services 24/7. Call the clinic number on this page and our team will guide you.

Can I see other specialists on the same visit?

Aneja Bhogal Multispeciality Hospital has eight departments, including orthopedics, general surgery, dermatology and physiotherapy, plus the Aneja Wellness Clinic.
