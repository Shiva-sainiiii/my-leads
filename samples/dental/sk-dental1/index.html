Good to know

Questions parents often ask.

Is the first check-up really free?

Yes. Your first consultation and check-up are free, and we explain everything before any treatment begins.

Will my child be scared or in pain?

We keep visits short and playful, use child-safe techniques and take every step at your child's pace.

How is the clinic sterilised?

Every instrument is sealed and autoclaved, and surfaces are disinfected between patients.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Yes. Call us for sudden pain, swelling or a broken tooth and we will make room the same day.
