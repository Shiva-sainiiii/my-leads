Questions patients often ask.

Will the treatment hurt?

We focus on comfortable, pain-free care with modern equipment, and Dr. Jyoti explains each step before starting.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Kids dentistry is one of our focus areas, with gentle, patient care at your child's pace.

Appointments are recommended so you wait less. Book on WhatsApp, use the form below or call us. We are open Mon to Sat 9:30 AM to 2 PM and 5:30 to 7:30 PM, and Sun 9:30 AM to 2 PM.

Yes. We welcome dental tourism patients who want international-standard care at an affordable cost. Message us to plan your visit.
