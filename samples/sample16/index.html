Easy to reach, easy to stay in

The hotel is a three-floor building near the Sheila Bypass Road and Delhi Road crossing, opposite Gulab Rewari. It is close to the bus stand and the railway station, so you can check in soon after you arrive.

Couples are welcome, and local IDs are accepted. The front desk is open 24 hours, and there is a lift and full power backup.

  • Local IDs accepted
  • 24-hour front desk
  • Free parking on site
